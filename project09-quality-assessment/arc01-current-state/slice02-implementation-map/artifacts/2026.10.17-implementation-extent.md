## Correction: environment-dependent native fixture

After the full native suite ran, a presence-only check showed that local
`priv/support.xsf` exists. `xs-run-graceful-when-no-xsf` calls `xs:run` and
accepts a successful story load as an alternative to missing-file fallback.
The native suite may therefore have incidentally loaded the local archive
through its priv path. The coordinator did not check this fixture boundary
before running the suite. Do not describe that run as entirely synthetic or
as proving the absent-archive path, and do not infer any prescribed real-archive
acceptance from its success. The initial tracked-file inventory hashed archive bytes as asset metadata;
no archive contents were semantically inspected or copied into reports. Targeted audit probes disable history/network
and use neutral fixtures; their evidence is separate from this suite caveat.
